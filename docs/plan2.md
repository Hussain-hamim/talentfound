**The idea: A platform where developers get discovered instead of applying.**

Instead of developers constantly applying to jobs, they create a **beautiful, modern developer profile** that acts like their professional identity.

Their profile can include:

* 👨‍💻 Bio & introduction
* 🛠️ Skills & tech stack
* 🚀 Projects with screenshots, demos and GitHub
* 💼 Experience
* 📄 Resume/CV
* 🔗 GitHub, LinkedIn, TikTok, personal website, etc.
* 🟢 Availability — "Open to work", "Open to freelance", "Open to co-founder opportunities", etc.

Then **recruiters and founders come to the platform and search for developers**.

They can filter by things like:

* React / Next.js / Python / AI / etc.
* Experience
* Location / remote
* Availability
* Type of work
* Skills

If they find someone they like, instead of the developer applying, **the recruiter/founder sends them an invitation or "Ping".**

For example:

> "Hey Alex, we saw your AI project and think you'd be a great fit for our startup. We'd love to talk."

So the main idea is:

**Traditional hiring:**
Developer → applies → company reviews → interview

**Our platform:**
Developer → showcases work → company discovers → company reaches out

---

### 🤝 Founder Matching

The platform would also have a second side:

**Find your co-founder / project partner.**

A developer could say:

> "I'm a full-stack developer looking for a founder with a SaaS idea."

A founder could say:

> "I'm building an AI startup and looking for a technical co-founder."

They can discover each other, match, and start a conversation.

So the platform isn't only for **getting hired** — it's also for **finding people to build something with.**

---

### 🎯 The bigger vision

We're essentially building a **professional network for builders**, focused on what people can actually build rather than just their résumé.

Think:

**GitHub + Portfolio + LinkedIn + Talent Marketplace + Co-founder matching**

but designed around **developers, projects and direct opportunities**.

The long-term vision is:

> **Don't apply for opportunities. Let opportunities find you.**

And developers could potentially have one profile that helps them find:
**Jobs + Freelance work + Co-founders + Startup projects + Collaborators.**
